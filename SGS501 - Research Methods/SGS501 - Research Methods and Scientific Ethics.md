## Course Overview: SGS 501/601 Research Methods and Scientific Ethics

* **Instructors:** The course is led by Dr. Arif Selçuk Öğrenci and Prof. Dr. Meltem Şengün Ucal.
* **Objectives:** The course aims to help students understand the research process, develop practical skills in statistics and time series analysis, and prepare ethical research proposals for thesis or grant programs.
* **Structure & Grading:** The semester spans 14 weeks and assessment relies on two quizzes (30%), three homework assignments (40%), and a final project (30%).
