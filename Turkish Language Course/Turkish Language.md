### 5. Practice Dialogue
**A:** Merhaba, benim adım Rüya, senin adın ne? _(Hello, my name is Rüya, what is your name?)_
**B:** Merhaba, benim adım Ahmet. _(Hello, my name is Ahmet.)_
**A:** Memnun oldum. _(Nice to meet you. / Lit: I became pleased.)_
**B:** Ben de memnun oldum. _(Nice to meet you too. / Lit: I also became pleased.)_
**A:** Nasılsın Ahmet? _(How are you Ahmet?)_
**B:** İyiyim, teşekkür ederim. Sen nasılsın? _(I'm fine, thank you. How are you?)_
**A:** Ben de iyiyim, teşekkürler. _(I'm fine too, thanks.)_
**B:** Nerelisin Rüya? _(Where are you from, Rüya?)_
**A:** Türkiyeliyim, sen nerelisin? _(I am from Turkey, where are you from?)_
**B:** Ben Suriyeliyim. _(I am from Syria.)_

### 6. Farewells (Vedalaşma)

| **Turkish**        | **English**    | **Usage Tip**                                       |
| ------------------ | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Görüşürüz**      | See you        | Casual, used every day.                             |
| **Görüşmek üzere** | See you (soon) | Slightly more formal/polite than _görüşürüz_.       |
| **Hoşça kal**      | Goodbye        | Said by the person **leaving**.                     |
| **Güle güle**      | Goodbye        | Said by the person **staying** (Lit: Go smilingly). |

**Language Tip:** Pay close attention to the dotted and undotted "i" (**i** vs **ı**). They make completely different sounds in Turkish. The "ı" sounds a bit like the "u" in the English word "cousin" or the "e" in "open".

### 3. Completing Your Sentence

You started writing a sentence about breakfast in Nigeria. Here is how you can correctly format and complete it:

**"Nijerya'da, ülkemde kahvaltıda..."**
_(In Nigeria, in my country, for breakfast...)_

**Example Completions:**
- **"...yumurta ve ekmek var."** (...there are eggs and bread.)
- **"...genellikle çay içeriz."** (...we usually drink tea.)

**Grammar Tip (The Locative Case "-da / -de / -ta / -te"):**

In your notes, you used **"ülkende"** (in your country) and **"kahvaltıda"** (at/for breakfast). The suffix **-de/-da** means "in," "on," or "at."

- _Ülke_ (country) + _n_ (your) + _de_ (in) = **Ülkende** (In your country)
- _Kahvaltı_ (breakfast) + _da_ (at) = **Kahvaltıda** (At breakfast)

### 1. Vowel Harmony (Vokal Harmoni)
Turkish vowels are split into two groups, which dictate suffix choices (-ler vs. -lar):
- **Back Vowels:** **a, o, u**
- **Front Vowels:** **e, i, ö, ü**

### 1. Question Suffixes & Vowel Harmony Rule
Depending on the last vowel of a word, the question particle changes to match:
* **a / ı** takes **mı**
* **e / i** takes **mi**
* **o / u** takes **mu**
* **ö / ü** takes **mü**

### 3. Affirming and Negating Questions (`Değil` = Not)

*Example pattern:* **Bu kitap mı?** — **Evet, bu kitap.** — **Hayır, bu kitap değil.** (Is this a book? — Yes, this is a book. — No, this is not a book.)
* **1. Şu çanta mı?** — Evet, şu çanta. — Hayır, şu çanta değil. (Is that a bag? — Yes, that's a bag. — No, that is not a bag.)
* **2. O ev mi?** — Evet, o ev. — Hayır, o ev değil. (Is that a house? — Yes, that's a house. — No, that is not a house.)
* **3. Şu şoför mü?** — Evet, şu şoför. — Hayır, şu şoför değil. (Is that a driver? — Yes, that's a driver. — No, that is not a driver.)
* **4. O hemşire mi?** — Evet, o hemşire. — Hayır, o hemşire değil. (Is she/he a nurse? — Yes, she/he is a nurse. — No, she/he is not a nurse.)
* **5. Şu perde mi?** — Evet, şu perde. — Hayır, şu perde değil. (Is that a curtain? — Yes, that's a curtain. — No, that is not a curtain.)
* **6. O futbolcu mu?** — Evet, o futbolcu. — Hayır, o futbolcu değil. (Is he/she a football player? — Yes, that's a football player. — No, that is not a football player.)
* **7. Bu kutu mu?** — Evet, bu kutu. — Hayır, bu kutu değil. (Is this a box? — Yes, this is a box. — No, that is not a box.)

### 1. Temel Fiil Kalıpları (Basic Verb Patterns)

* **[Ne/Türkçe] biliyor musun?** — Do you know [what/Turkish]?
* **Evet, anlıyorum.** — Yes, I understand.
* **Hayır, anlamıyorum.** — No, I don't understand.
* **[Ne/Kimi] seviyor musun?** — Do you love/like [what/who]?
* **Evet, seviyorum.** — Yes, I love/like (it).
* **Hayır, sevmiyorum.** — No, I don't love/like (it).
* **Ne istiyorsun? / [Ne] istiyor musun?** — What do you want? / Do you want [something]?
* **Evet, istiyorum.** — Yes, I want (it).
* **Hayır, istemiyorum.** — No, I don't want (it).
